# Desktop Gang 🐧⚡

![Meet the gang: Volt, Penguin and Volt 2 moved into my taskbar](assets/meet-the-gang.webp)

Three little characters that live on your Windows taskbar: **Volt**, **Penguin** and **Volt 2**.
They walk around on top of everything, react to your PC, dance to your music and go home to sleep.

## What they do

- **Live on your taskbar** and walk around on their own. Drag one anywhere.
- **Right-click** for the menu: size (Small, Medium, Large, Huge), switch character, stay put,
  ghost mode (your clicks go straight through), and Sleep.
- **React to your PC**, live: they sweat when the CPU is cooking and get excited when a game starts.
  Hover the tray icon for CPU, GPU and temperature.
- **Music mode:** when Spotify (or any music app) plays, they grab their headphones and dance,
  with a little "♪ Title – Artist" bubble.
- **Go home:** Penguin to his igloo, the robots to their charging dock. Sometimes just for a little break.
- **Stay out of your way:** maximize an app and they let your clicks through.

Every character has a secret. Leave your PC alone for 10 minutes and see where they go.
